When a cat licks the gravy and leaves the pieces, an empty-looking pouch can give a misleading impression of how much dinner went in. Start with a complete cat food labelled for the right life stage, then look at which texture your cat actually eats. The five examples below cover dry and wet formats available through Indian product ranges.

## Compare these five options

| Food | A reason to consider it | A useful observation at home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whiskas Kitten Dry Food, Mackerel | A growing kitten already accustomed to kibble | Measure what goes into the bowl and check what is left. Keep adult housemates out of the kitten's meals. |
| Royal Canin Kitten Instinctive in Gravy | A kitten that takes small, soft pieces readily | Watch whether it eats the pieces as well as the liquid. Follow the kitten chart on the local pack. |
| Whiskas Adult Dry Food, Tuna | A healthy adult cat that prefers a crunchy ration | Set aside a measured daily amount. Food used in puzzle feeders comes out of that same portion. |
| Whiskas Adult Tuna in Jelly | An adult that likes a more cohesive wet texture | Try one pouch before buying a case. Serve and store opened food according to the pack. |
| Royal Canin Instinctive in Gravy, adult | An adult already accustomed to morsels with sauce | Use the adult product. The similarly named kitten food has a different feeding guide. |

## How the foods were chosen

The manufacturers' Indian ranges identify the species, life stage and form. These options show contrasts an owner can test at mealtime. Flavour preference, price and availability vary. WSAVA recommends looking beyond the front of the bag to the adequacy statement, manufacturer expertise and energy density. Check the actual label each time a product name, pack size or recipe changes. A complementary topper and a complete wet food can look remarkably similar once poured into a bowl.

## A feeding record that works with two cats

For one observed meal, put the bowls in separate rooms. Record the food, the amount offered, how much came back and which cat ate it. With wet food, check soon after the meal so drying does not distort the comparison. If one cat finishes both bowls overnight, dividing the household total by two will miss a change in the other cat. A kitchen scale and a brief note can make that change visible to the vet.

## Can I mix dry and wet?

Yes, when the foods fit the cat's life stage and medical needs and their portions fit the daily plan. Use the maker's mixed-feeding chart if available. A dry-food scoop and a pouch contain different amounts of water and energy. For a cat on a veterinary urinary or kidney diet, ask before adding another formula. If your cat stops eating, especially with vomiting or lethargy, contact your veterinarian promptly. Waiting for a different flavour to arrive can delay needed care.

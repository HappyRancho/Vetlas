For a planned referral, the most useful hospital is one that accepts your animal, has the team you need and can arrange follow-up close to home. A charitable centre for injured street animals and a specialist surgical hospital answer quite different calls. These five institutions publish their own service information. We selected them for the different referral needs they serve.

## Five services, five reasons to call

### Small Animal Hospital Mumbai

Mahalaxmi, Mumbai. Specialist companion-animal care. Its soft-tissue service lists abdominal, urinary and reconstructive procedures. Ask where to send imaging and which team can accept the case.

[Branch details and directions](/mumbai/tata-trusts-small-animal-hospital-mahalaxmi-saat-rasta/)

### Madras Veterinary College Teaching Hospital

Vepery, Chennai. Large and small animals at a university hospital. Ask which clinical department runs the required service and whether registration and the specialist consultation happen on the same day.

[Vepery hospital profile](/chennai/madras-veterinary-college-teaching-hospital-tanuvas-vepery/)

### ICAR–IVRI Referral Veterinary Polyclinic

Izatnagar, Bareilly. Referral and teaching clinical complex. IVRI publishes information on facilities including endoscopy. Have your veterinarian describe the clinical question before you arrange the journey.

### Jivdaya Charitable Trust

Ambawadi, Ahmedabad. Unowned domestic animals and strays, with rehabilitation for birds, reptiles and small mammals. For an injured free-living animal, call with the precise location and species or description. The trust can advise on intake or mobile help.

[Ambawadi facility and rescue contacts](/ahmedabad/jivdaya-charitable-trust-animal-hospital-ambawadi-panjrapol-campus/)

### Bai Sakarbai Dinshaw Petit Hospital for Animals

Parel, Mumbai. Animal hospital with an outpatient department run with Bombay Veterinary College. The hospital lists digital radiography, inpatient wards and ambulance services. Ask the admissions office about the right entrance and current intake.

## How to make the first call count

Give the species, approximate weight, current problem and your location before describing a preferred specialist. Ask the person answering to repeat the branch name. In a city with several campuses, a hospital's teaching department and its emergency entrance can be at different addresses. Obtain specific instructions for arrival, especially for a large animal or a patient that cannot walk.

For an owned pet, ask whether referral paperwork, registration or an advance deposit is required. For a rescued animal, clarify who can authorise treatment and who will provide transport and follow-up. Call a wildlife service before trying to handle or move a wild animal.

## Compare what happens after the visit

An owner travelling for an imaging appointment may need only a report and copies of the images. A patient undergoing surgery may need inpatient monitoring, a local dressing change, and a named contact if recovery changes overnight. Write down who will send the discharge summary and whether the referring clinic can take over rechecks. If an animal becomes unstable before departure, contact the nearest available emergency team first.

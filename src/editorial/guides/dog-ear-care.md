Dark wax, a smell and head shaking can occur with several ear problems. The colour alone cannot identify yeast, bacteria, mites or a foreign body. If an ear is painful, red, suddenly troublesome on one side or associated with a head tilt or balance change, contact a veterinarian before putting anything in it.

## Get the instructions in the right order
After an examination, ask which ear to treat, whether cleaning is needed, which product to use and how long to separate cleaning from medication. Some products are unsuitable when the eardrum is damaged. Keep left and right instructions separate if they differ. A previously opened bottle from another episode may be the wrong treatment this time.

## If your vet has prescribed cleaning
Prepare the cleaner, gauze or cotton and a towel before calling your dog over. Follow the demonstrated technique and product instructions. Avoid touching the bottle tip to the canal. Allow the dog to shake and gently wipe material that comes into easy reach. Do not probe with cotton buds, push material deeper or improvise with peroxide or alcohol. If handling hurts, stop and ask the clinic how to proceed.

## Check the response, not just the smell
Record head shaking, sleep disturbance, scratching and comfort when the outer ear is touched. A better smell does not show that the canal has recovered. Attend the planned recheck, particularly after recurring episodes. The team may inspect the canal and examine a sample under a microscope to decide what to continue.

**Should a healthy ear be cleaned on a schedule?** Some dogs need little or no routine cleaning. Ask for advice based on your individual dog, previous infections and activities such as swimming. Excessive cleaning can irritate an otherwise comfortable ear.

<details class="story-faq">
<summary>Why does the vet want another sample when the scratching stops?</summary>
<p>Signs can settle before the canal has recovered. In a recurring case, looking at debris under the microscope can help the clinician check what organisms and inflammation are present now. Ask whether the ear should be left uncleaned before that appointment so the examination answers the intended question.</p>

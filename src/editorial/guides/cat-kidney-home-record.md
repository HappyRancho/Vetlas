A single water-bowl measurement can be misleading. Another cat may drink from it, wet food contributes water, and evaporation matters. For chronic kidney disease, the useful record is often a pattern of changes connected to the cat you know, interpreted alongside weight, examination, blood pressure, blood tests and urine results.

## Pick observations you can repeat
Offer the usual measured food and record what remains. Note vomiting, litter clump changes, interest in familiar activities and body weight if your vet recommends home weighing. Use the same scale and similar conditions. If your cat is impossible to weigh calmly, tell the team and arrange clinic measurements. A household with several cats may need separate supervised meals before anyone can interpret a half-empty dish.

## Make eating and drinking easier
Keep fresh water in several accessible places. Ask the clinic how to transition to a prescribed diet, what to do if the cat rejects it, and when poor intake warrants a call. A cat that stops eating needs prompt advice. Do not keep withholding acceptable food while waiting for a reluctant cat to accept a new diet. Fluid treatment, phosphate binders and other medicines need an individual prescription and monitoring.

## Bring the record to the recheck
Three lines can be enough: “Monday finished most of both meals. Tuesday licked gravy only and vomited once. Wednesday did not come to breakfast.” Include medication times and what actually went in. A number without its units or a list of intended doses can confuse the discussion.

**Does more drinking mean I should limit water?** Keep water available. A kidney patient may need to drink more because the kidneys cannot concentrate urine effectively. New weakness, repeated vomiting or a marked change in intake warrants a call before the routine review date.

<details class="story-faq">
<summary>What if the laboratory reports come from different clinics?</summary>
<p>Keep the full reports with dates, units and reference intervals, and identify which visit included fluid treatment. Bring any urine and blood pressure results from the same period. Two isolated creatinine numbers written down without these details leave the receiving vet much less information for judging a trend.</p>
</details>

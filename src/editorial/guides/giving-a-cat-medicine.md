A handwritten tick beside “evening medicine” can mean either “I prepared it” or “the cat swallowed it.” Agree what a tick means before the first dose. Keep the dispensed container with the label and use one shared record. Record the time after administration, the person giving it and any uncertainty.

## Choose the method with the clinic
Ask whether this specific tablet can be split or crushed, whether food changes its absorption, and whether it needs a food or water follow-up. Some tablets can injure a cat’s oesophagus if they lodge there. Ask the team to demonstrate a suitable technique. Never force a stream of water into the throat. If using food is permitted, use a small separate portion so you can tell whether the dose was eaten. Keep the main meal familiar.

## Measure liquids carefully
Read the strength and the volume on the dispensing label together. Use the supplied oral syringe, check its units, and ask whether the suspension needs shaking. With the cat upright, introduce small amounts at the side of the mouth and allow swallowing. Stop if the cat coughs, struggles or cannot swallow comfortably. Ask for a different formulation or a clinic demonstration if administration repeatedly fails.

| What happened | What to write and do |
| --- | --- |
| Another caregiver gave the dose | Record their actual time before preparing anything else. |
| Cat spat out an unknown amount | Record what you saw and call for advice before repeating. |
| Dose was missed or vomited | Check the medicine-specific plan with the prescriber. |

## Can a second bottle replace the first?
Even bottles with the same drug name may have different concentrations. At each refill, compare the new label and syringe with the instructions. Discard obsolete schedules so the next caregiver sees one unambiguous plan.

<summary>What should we leave for a pet sitter?</summary>
<p>Leave one current, dated schedule with the clinic’s number, the labelled packs and a place for the sitter to enter actual times. Have them show you how they read the syringe or tablet instructions before you leave. Write down the clinic’s advice for missed doses for each medicine separately.</p>
